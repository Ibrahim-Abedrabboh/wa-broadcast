## Recording Instructions

### Screen Recording Setup
1. **Resolution:** 1920x1080 or 2560x1440
2. **Browser:** Chrome with dark mode
3. **Language:** Set to Arabic (ar) for RTL demo
4. **Tool:** OBS, ScreenFlow, or Loom

### Pages to Record
1. `/dashboard` — show stats and charts
2. `/inbox` — show two-way conversation
3. `/contacts` — show contact list
4. `/templates` — show template builder
5. `/settings` — show client switcher (if SUPERADMIN)

### Music
- Background: Lo-fi or ambient tech music (royalty-free)
- Volume: 20-30% under voiceover

### Tools
- **Recording:** OBS Studio (free) or ScreenFlow
- **Editing:** DaVinci Resolve (free) or iMovie
- **Music:** Pixabay Music or Uppbeat (free tier)

### Export Settings
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264)
- **Resolution:** 1080p
- **Frame rate:** 30fps
- **Bitrate:** 8-12 Mbps
