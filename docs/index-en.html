Key Features

🏢

Multi-Tenancy

Each client gets their own WhatsApp number, contacts, templates, and team. Perfect for marketing agencies.

💬

Two-Way Inbox

Reply to customers directly in live conversations with 24-hour service window tracking and media support.

🎨

Rich Templates

Media headers (image/video/PDF), coupon codes, carousels, and interactive buttons (call, URL, quick reply).

📊

Detailed Analytics

Delivery funnel (Sent → Delivered → Read), read rates, button event tracking, and per-campaign stats.

📅

Campaign Scheduling

Recurring and scheduled campaigns with UTC timezone support, pause and resume capabilities.

🔒

Security & Compliance

Full encryption (bcrypt, JWT), HMAC webhook verification, rate limiting. Ideal for Saudi PDPL, UAE DPL compliance.

Who Is It For?

  • Digital marketing companies
  • Social media agencies
  • Technology solution providers (SaaS)
  • Companies managing WhatsApp for multiple clients
  • Any business wanting a professional WhatsApp solution without limits

Start in 5 Minutes

git clone https://github.com/Ibrahim-Abedrabboh/wa-broadcast
cd wa-broadcast
cp .env.example .env
docker compose up --build

That's it! Platform running at http://localhost:3000
